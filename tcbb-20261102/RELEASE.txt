asem-refine-benchmarks v1.5.0 — 30-species CIRR benchmark and strand-aware follow-up
Release date: 2 November 2026

This folder preserves the complete contents of the author-approved archive
cirr-panel30-strand-aware-20261102-candidate.zip, whose SHA-256 is
5ac0804a28d239d3649bc273b45fc459ccf0966f1415b93a9e8c2f2c95a5ca8d.
The ZIP is also attached to the GitHub release. SHA256SUMS verifies the candidate
contents; RELEASE.txt is the only added file in this folder.

Main grid: 30 species, 870 ordered pairs, depths 1–8X, three replicates,
eight methods, 167,040 rows. Seven methods are in results/main_grid_seven_methods.csv;
MITObim is in results/mitobim_scored.csv. Accuracy remains conditional on output.
Separate main/ and followup/ snapshots preserve the forward-only main experiment
and the strand-aware follow-up. No reruns or numerical revisions accompany release.

The bundled README.txt and S1 Text reflect preparation before publication and
therefore contain pending-release wording. That wording is historical for this
archived snapshot. The current manuscript and supplement will cite the published
version after the DOI is minted. S3/S4 retain explicitly historical diagnostics.

Mus musculus has family Muridae in taxonomy/panel30_species.py and S1 Table.
Frozen snapshots retain the original Muroidea label for provenance. All 870
pair tiers are unchanged by this correction; see taxonomy/correction.json.

The public empirical BAM and retained per-job assembly/work directories are not
included. INPUTS_NOT_BUNDLED.json supplies input hashes; README.txt describes
reproduction limits and environment/machine-path requirements. SOURCE_HASHES.json
records source and packaged hashes, including one metadata-only path replacement.
